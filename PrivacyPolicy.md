  1. Overview                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
ResumeAI is a resume builder application for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly what data is collected, why, and how it is used. ResumeAI does not require an account, does not require sign-in, and stores all your resume data locally on your device.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2. Data We Collect and Why                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.1 Resume Content (Stored Locally)                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  When you create a resume, you enter information such as your name, email, phone number, address, work experience, education, skills, projects,  and achievements. This data is stored exclusively in a local database on your device.  
  It is never uploaded to our servers.                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.2 Profile Photo                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  The app requests camera permission to let you take a profile photo for your resume. You may also select a photo from your gallery. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to any server by us.              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.3 AI Writing Assistance                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  When you use AI features (generating objectives, improving job descriptions, suggesting skills, writing project descriptions, or improving achievements), the specific text you submit for that request is sent to Groq, Inc. via     
  their API to generate suggestions. Only the text you explicitly submit to the AI feature is sent — no other resume data leaves your device.                                                                                           
  Groq's Privacy Policy: https://groq.com/privacy                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.4 Advertising                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  ResumeAI displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:               
  https://policies.google.com/privacy                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:                                                                                                                                                                                      
  Device Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalisation                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.5 Network & Version Check                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  The app checks the Play Store to detect if a newer version is available. This uses your internet connection and sends only a standard HTTP request to the Play Store — no personal data is included.                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  2.6 Exported Files (PDF & Word)                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  PDF files are generated entirely on your device using Android's built-in PDF engine. Word (.doc) files are generated locally from your resume content. Both are saved directly to your device's Downloads folder. No file is uploaded 
  to any server.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        

  3. Permissions Used                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  INTERNET — AI writing features (Groq API), AdMob ads, Play Store version check                                                                                                                                                        
  CAMERA — Take a profile photo for your resume                                                                                                                                                                                         
  ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Check if internet is available before attempting AI or ad requests                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  4. Data Sharing                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. The only third parties that may receive data are:                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Groq Inc. — receives only the text you submit to AI features                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Google AdMob — receives device/ad identifiers for ad serving                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Both are governed by their own privacy policies linked above.                                                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  5. Data Storage & Security                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  All resume data is stored in an encrypted local Room database on your device. We operate no servers that hold your personal information. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored resume data.

  7. Your Rights & Controls                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  Since all resume data lives on your device, you have full control:                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Edit or delete any resume directly in the app                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Delete all data by uninstalling the app                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Opt out of personalised ads via Google device settings                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Disable AI features by simply not using them (no data is sent unless you tap an AI button)

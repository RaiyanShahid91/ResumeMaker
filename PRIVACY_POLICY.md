# Privacy Policy — ResumeAI

_Last updated: 2026-08-04_

ResumeAI ("the app") is developed by Raiyan Shahid. This policy explains what data the app
handles and why.

## No account required

ResumeAI works without requiring sign-in or an account. Your resumes and cover letters are
created and stored locally on your device. Building, editing, previewing, and exporting a resume
all work without an internet connection.

## What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — personal details, education, work experience, skills, projects, photos,
and your signature — is saved in the app's private local storage on your device. We do not
operate a server that stores your resume content.

## AI features and what they send

Optional AI features — writing suggestions, cover letter generation, the AI assistant, and
importing a resume from a PDF or photo — send the relevant text (including text read from an
imported file) to [Groq](https://groq.com), our third-party AI provider, solely to generate a
response. This only happens when you actively use an AI feature; it does not happen
automatically. Groq processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://groq.com/privacy.

## Analytics, crash reporting & ads

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand feature usage and
diagnose crashes, and Google AdMob to show ads, which may use your advertising ID. These services
can collect device, usage, and diagnostic data as described in Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy. This does not include the content of your resume or cover
letter.

## Permissions we ask for

Camera and photo/file access are used so you can add a profile photo or signature, scan a
document, or import an existing resume from a photo or PDF. Files you pick are read only to fill
in your resume and are not uploaded anywhere except as described above for AI import.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a resume or cover letter in the app removes it, its photos, and its saved AI assistant
conversation from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally, since we
don't keep a server-side copy.

## Children's privacy

ResumeAI is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most
recent revision. Material changes will be reflected in an updated in-app version of this policy
as well.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent via the "Write Us" option in the app's
Settings screen.
